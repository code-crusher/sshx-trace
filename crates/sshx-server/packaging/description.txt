sshx is a secure web-based, collaborative terminal.
.
Run a single command to share your terminal with anyone. Users can resize,
move windows, and freely zoom and pan on an infinite canvas, see other
people's cursors moving in real time, and connect to the nearest server in a
globally distributed mesh.
.
Sessions are end-to-end encrypted with Argon2 and AES, with automatic
reconnection and real-time latency estimates. Predictive echo enables faster
local editing, a la Mosh.
